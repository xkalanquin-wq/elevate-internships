## Full description

Elevate Internships helps college business students find real internships and
build the credentials employers look for, all free.

FIND INTERNSHIPS NEAR YOU
Enter your ZIP code and major to search real internship listings from more
than 300 employer career sites and trusted job feeds. Results are ranked
by distance and fit. Filter by season, in-person or remote, and see which
listings are new this week. One tap also opens LinkedIn, Handshake, and
Indeed with your search already filled in.

TRACK EVERY APPLICATION
Save internships and move them from Saved to Applied, Interviewing, and
Offer. Add deadlines and interview times, export them to your calendar, and
keep notes on each one. Found a role somewhere else? Add it by hand.

CERTIFICATIONS FOR YOUR MAJOR
More than 200 certifications across 23 business majors: accounting, finance,
marketing, business analytics, supply chain, management, and more. Each has
honest pricing (many are free), a starter set to begin with, and a ledger
that tracks what you've finished.

KNOW WHEN TO APPLY
Every major has a recruiting timeline showing when firms hire, so you apply
before the deadlines pass, not after.

REMINDERS AND ALERTS
Turn on notifications and get a reminder the day before an application
deadline or interview. A weekly email can also send new internships that
match your search.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads in the app and no third-party analytics. You can use everything without
an account. Sign up only if you want your ledger and applications on every
device.

Listings come from third parties. Always confirm details on the employer's
own site before applying.
