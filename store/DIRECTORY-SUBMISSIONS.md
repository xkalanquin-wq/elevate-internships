# Directory submission kit

Everything to paste when listing Elevate Internships on directories. Each site needs its own account, which the owner creates; nothing here is submitted automatically.

## Where to list it (free)

Local-business directories (Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Yelp, BBB, Yellow Pages, Apple Business Connect locations, Foursquare) are for businesses that serve customers at an address. An online-only app does not qualify, and listing one anyway risks suspension, so they are left out on purpose.

These fit a free web app for students and help both search engines and AI assistants recognise the name:

| Where | Why | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bing Webmaster Tools | Bing search, and the AI assistants that use Bing | Import from Google Search Console. IndexNow submissions already run on every deploy. |
| LinkedIn company page | The profile recruiters, students and AI tools check first | Name: Elevate Internships. Add the logo, the website and the long description. |
| Crunchbase | Company profile widely read by data providers and AI tools | Free basic profile. |
| Product Hunt | Launch day audience; the listing stays up and is indexed | Pick a weekday; ask a few friends to try it that day. |
| AlternativeTo | Lists it as an alternative to Handshake, Indeed and LinkedIn Jobs | Add it as an alternative to each. |
| SaaSHub | Software directory with alternatives pages | Free listing. |
| BetaList | Early-stage product directory | Free listing has a waiting queue; skip the paid expedite. |
| Uneed, Launching Next | Small launch directories | Free. |
| Microsoft Store | Already submitted | Add the store link to LinkedIn and Crunchbase once live. |
| Campus career centers | Resource pages students actually use | Email the career center; see the outreach note below. |

## Tagline (under 60 characters)

> Find, track and land business internships — free.

## Short description (about 160 characters)

> Free internship finder and tracker for business majors: search internships by ZIP code, track applications to an offer, and see the certifications for your field.

## Long description

> Elevate Internships helps college students in business majors find and land internships. Search real internship listings by ZIP code and distance — including roles posted only on companies' own career pages on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and SmartRecruiters — ranked by how well they fit your major.
>
> Every major, from accounting and finance to supply chain, human resources and real estate, gets its own recruiting timeline, resume keywords and a list of the certifications worth adding, labelled honestly as free, free to audit or paid.
>
> Track each application from saved to applied, interviewing and offer, with deadlines, interview dates, calendar export and optional reminder emails. Turn on a weekly email to hear about new internships that match your search.
>
> It is free, you can search without an account, and it works on a phone or laptop or as an installed app.
